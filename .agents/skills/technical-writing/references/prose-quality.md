# Prose quality

Apply this pass after the technical claims and page structure are correct. The goal is clear, natural eve documentation, not a generic rewrite.

## Preserve intent

- Preserve supported meaning, facts, nuance, and necessary uncertainty.
- Keep established eve terminology even when a synonym would add variety.
- Leave strong sentences alone. Make the minimum effective edit.
- Preserve useful detail instead of replacing it with broad summaries.
- Do not invent examples, measurements, sources, opinions, reactions, or implementation details.
- Match eve's neutral instructional voice rather than an individual author's casual voice.

## Improve clarity

- Lead with the point when setup adds no necessary context.
- Prefer concrete nouns and direct verbs over abstractions and inflated claims.
- Cut repetition, filler, and empty qualifiers without deleting real constraints.
- Untangle sentences that require rereading, but keep clear long sentences when they carry necessary detail.
- Vary rhythm only when repeated sentence shapes sound mechanical.

## Inspect contextual warning signs

Treat these as warnings, not mechanical bans. Keep an intentional construction when it is the clearest choice or matches repository style.

- **Unsupported importance:** Remove claims that something is pivotal, transformative, or broadly significant unless evidence supports the consequence.
- **Promotional language:** Replace praise such as seamless, robust, groundbreaking, or powerful with concrete behavior.
- **Superficial analysis:** Remove trailing clauses such as “highlighting” or “underscoring” when they do not explain a mechanism or consequence.
- **Vague attribution:** Name an authoritative supplied source or remove phrases such as “experts agree” and “studies show.”
- **Stock rhetoric:** Remove throat-clearing, faux-insight setups, self-answered questions, decorative contrasts, negative lists, and colon reveals when the following statement works alone.
- **Decorative rhythm:** Avoid stacked fragments, forced groups of three, false ranges, repeated sentence templates, and punctuation used only for emphasis.
- **Mechanical diction:** Replace fake-strong verbs, synonym cycling, swollen phrases, stacked hedging, and generic AI vocabulary with the precise term.
- **Chatbot artifacts:** Remove canned praise, offers to continue, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, and “I hope this helps” language.
- **Weak endings:** End on the last useful fact or next action instead of a redundant summary, slogan, metaphor, or “In conclusion” paragraph.
- **Decorative formatting:** Remove scattered emphasis, unnecessary micro-headings, and lists that are clearer as short prose.

## Final read

Read the changed section in full, then check:

1. Every claim still matches its source.
2. The revision preserves the original intent and necessary uncertainty.
3. Each sentence contributes information, reasoning, or a necessary transition.
4. The prose sounds direct and natural when read aloud.
5. The edit did not normalize unrelated or already-clear writing.

For a review-only request, report concise findings with a clickable file and line. Do not assign an AI score or claim to know whether AI wrote the text.
