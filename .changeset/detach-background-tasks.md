Workflow tools accept `detach: true`: the call returns a receipt at once (`{ status: "working", taskId }` on `action.result`), and the run's result reaches the model later in one `<task_result>` message, within the turn that started it: a turn cannot end while tasks it started are working, so eve holds it and calls the model again as results arrive. An interactive root turn shows `turn.completed` and `session.waiting` while it holds and stays open under the same `turnId`; the default client reducer starts a new assistant message after each boundary and marks the closed one with `metadata.closed`. Background tasks stay listed in the `[Tasks]` note until their results arrive, a session holds at most 10 working ones (`TOO_MANY_BACKGROUND_TASKS`), and subagents and task-mode runs reply only after their tasks settle. The stream marks delivered results as `message.received` with `kind: "task.result"` and `taskIds`, which the client reducer and built-in channels do not render as a user message. A child session that ends before replying now fails its call with `AGENT_SESSION_ENDED`.
