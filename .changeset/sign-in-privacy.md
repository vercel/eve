Built-in channels show a sign-in's link or code only where the person signing in alone reads it, and a link-free status elsewhere. Teams group chats and channels now ask the person to continue in a direct message, Discord shows sign-ins (the link in a one-to-one DM, a status in servers and group DMs), and GitHub, where every comment is public, posts a status saying the person who asked must sign in.
