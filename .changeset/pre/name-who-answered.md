Chat SDK, Discord, Telegram, and Teams now say who answered a question or approval when they edit its prompt, as Slack already does: `Approved by Alice` or `Answered by Alice: Saturday`.

Each channel records the person behind a principal when that principal sends a message or presses a button, then names them from the settlement's responder. Discord shows a mention, which doesn't notify anyone because the edit allows no mentions. Teams already named who pressed Approve; it now also names people who approve by text or answer questions. A prompt withdrawn without an answer, or answered by someone the channel never saw, reads as before.
