Slack sign-in prompts now say which service they are for and why. The private message reads `Connect Linear: To continue, I need access to your Linear account` with a `Connect Linear` button, and a device code, when the provider issues one, appears as a short hint below the button instead of leading with `Use code … when prompted`. The thread status names who it is waiting on, as in `Waiting for @alice to connect Linear…`.
