# Mastodon best practices

A sourced checklist of current tactics for writing on Mastodon. Use it as a pre-flight check, not a recipe: the house voice (first-person, plain, concrete) wins over any single rule.

## Voice & audience

- [ ] Write as a community member, not a brand. The fediverse has no ads and no algorithm to push content; people come for conversation, not to be marketed to.
- [ ] Lead with value or a genuine question. Text and dialogue carry more than visuals here.
- [ ] Skip announcement voice and growth-hacking. "People don't typically like to be marketed to": sales-first posts fall flat.
- [ ] Don't expect a Twitter/X mindset to transfer. Treat Mastodon as its own space.

## Length & format

- [ ] Stay near the 500-character default. Some instances allow more, but 500 is the norm to write for. No need to fill it.

## Links

- [ ] Link directly to the source. Every link counts as 23 characters regardless of length.
- [ ] Don't use a link shortener: Mastodon's docs actively discourage it, and it adds nothing since links are already counted as a flat 23.

## Media & accessibility

- [ ] Add alt text (image description) to every image. Screen readers and assistive tech use it; it also shows when media fails to load.
- [ ] Write hashtags in CamelCase (`#WebDev`, `#DogsOfMastodon`), not `#webdev`. Without capitals, screen readers read multi-word tags as one gibberish word. This is expected etiquette, not a nicety (the "curb cut effect": it helps everyone).

## Content warnings (CW)

- [ ] Use a CW to collapse sensitive, spoiler, or long content behind a short label. Readers see only the label and click to expand.
- [ ] Treat the CW like a subject line when useful: it gives people control over what they read. Adding a CW also marks attached media as sensitive (blurred until clicked).
- [ ] Don't overuse it for ordinary posts; reserve it for content people may want to opt into.

## Hashtags & discovery

- [ ] Include a few topical hashtags. By default, public posts surface in search only via their hashtags: full-text search exists but is opt-in, so untagged posts are largely invisible to topic searches.
- [ ] Use a handful, not a wall. Place them anywhere; position doesn't affect function.
- [ ] Don't use numbers-only hashtags: they won't register.

## Feed & timing

- [ ] Remember the feed is chronological. There's no algorithm boosting reach, so a post is seen by followers who happen to be online: write each post to stand on its own.
