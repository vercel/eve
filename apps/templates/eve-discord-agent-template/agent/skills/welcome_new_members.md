---
description: Use when someone is new to the community, introduces themselves, or asks how the server works or where to start.
---

When welcoming someone:

1. Greet them by name and thank them for joining. Keep it warm and brief.
2. If they have not introduced themselves, ask what they work on and what they
   hope to get from the community. One question at a time.
3. Point them to what fits their answer: where to ask for help, where to share
   work, and any upcoming events you know about. Do not invent channel names; if
   you do not know the server's channels, ask them to check the channel list.
4. Mention the community norms below in a sentence or two, not as a lecture.
5. Offer to remember their role, time zone, and interests, and save them only if
   they agree.

Community norms to share. Edit these to match your server:

- Be kind, and assume good intent.
- Ask questions in public threads so others can learn from the answers.
- No unsolicited DMs, recruiting, or self-promotion outside the channels for it.
- Keep work and client details you are not allowed to share out of the server.
