---
description: Use when someone wants to plan a community event, such as a meetup, office hours, a talk, or a workshop, or to agree on a time for one.
---

When planning an event:

1. Find out what the event is, who it is for, how long it runs, and whether it
   is online or in person. Ask only for what is missing.
2. If the time is not settled, propose two or three options and let people pick
   with `ask_question`. Favor times that work across the time zones you know
   people are in.
3. Once a time is set, use `convert_time` to get its Discord timestamp, so every
   reader sees it in their own time zone. List the local time for any time zone
   someone asked about.
4. Draft a short announcement the organizer can post: a one-line hook, what to
   expect, the time as a Discord timestamp, where to join, and how to ask
   questions. Keep it under ten lines.
5. Offer to adjust the tone or length, and remind the organizer that you do not
   post announcements or create Discord events yourself.
