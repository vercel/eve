---
description: Use when someone asks what they missed, wants a recap or summary of a channel or thread, or asks what was decided.
---

When catching someone up:

1. Call `read_recent_messages` to read the channel or thread they asked in. Read
   more messages if the conversation they're asking about started earlier.
2. Lead with the answer to what they asked. If they only asked what they missed,
   open with the one or two things that matter most.
3. Then list, in short bullets, only what applies:
   - **Decisions**: what was agreed, and who agreed it.
   - **Open questions**: what is still unresolved.
   - **Action items**: who said they would do what.
4. Name people the way they appear in the channel, without pinging them.
5. Keep the whole recap under ten lines, and skip small talk and your own earlier
   messages. If nothing important happened, say so in one sentence.
